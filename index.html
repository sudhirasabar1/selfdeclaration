What is a Self Declaration Form?

A Self Declaration Form is a legally binding document where an individual declares certain facts about themselves. It is often required for government schemes, educational admissions, and official paperwork when a specific certificate (like an income or caste certificate) is not available or is pending.

Common Uses:

  • Income Declaration: To declare family income for scholarships or fee waivers.
  • Caste Declaration: To declare one's caste category for reservation benefits.
  • Residence Declaration: To provide proof of address for school admissions or government forms.

Note: This tool generates a standard format. Always verify if the receiving authority requires a specific format or notarization.
